About the Portal

The Semester Registration Portal is a dedicated digital ecosystem designed for the students of the Indian Institute of Engineering Science and Technology, Shibpur.

Established in 1856, IIEST Shibpur is an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. This secure platform facilitates seamless semester enrollment, academic record management, and fee status tracking, ensuring transparency and administrative efficiency.
